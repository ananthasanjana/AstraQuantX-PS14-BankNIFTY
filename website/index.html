-> Headline result: DUAL + Random Forest <--

Why VWAP + Bollinger Bands work together

Each indicator covers the other's blind spot. The Random Forest only accepts or rejects signals; it is not a third indicator.

Direction
VWAP

Price above the session VWAP = buyers in control. Only CE trades are allowed above VWAP, only PE below.

Timing
Bollinger Bands

Wait for a pullback: z-score dips to −0.5σ and turns back up (mirror for PE). Enter on a confirmed bar close.

Filter
Random Forest  p̂ ≥ τ

300 trees retrained every session on past signals only. Acts when the predicted chance of hitting +12% before −6% is at least 50%.

Mathematical formulation

1 · VWAP (resets daily)
\mathrm{VWAP}_t=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{t} TP_i V_i}{\sum_{i=1}^{t} V_i},\qquad TP_i=\frac{H_i+L_i+C_i}{3}

i = first bar of the session.

2 · Bollinger Bands (n = 20, k = 2)
MA_t=\frac1n\sum_{i=t-n+1}^{t} C_i,\quad \sigma_t=\sqrt{\frac1n\sum_{i=t-n+1}^{t}(C_i-MA_t)^2}
U_t=MA_t+k\sigma_t,\quad L_t=MA_t-k\sigma_t,\quad BW_t=\frac{U_t-L_t}{MA_t}
3 · z-score and VWAP distance
z_t=\frac{C_t-MA_t}{\sigma_t},\qquad d_t=\frac{C_t-\mathrm{VWAP}_t}{\mathrm{VWAP}_t}

Both feed the Random Forest.

4 · Black-Scholes ATM premium
d_1=\frac{\ln(S/K)+(r+\tfrac12\sigma^2)T}{\sigma\sqrt T},\quad d_2=d_1-\sigma\sqrt T
C=S\Phi(d_1)-Ke^{-rT}\Phi(d_2),\quad P=Ke^{-rT}\Phi(-d_2)-S\Phi(-d_1)

K = nearest 100, σ = 15%, r = 6%, T = 4 days at entry.

5 · Random Forest filter
\hat p_t=\frac1B\sum_{b=1}^{B}h_b(\mathbf x_t),\qquad \text{execute iff } \hat p_t\ge\tau

B = 300 trees, τ = 0.5. Label y = 1 if the premium hits +12% before −6% within 12 bars.

6 · Position size and Sharpe
\text{lots}=\Big\lfloor\frac{0.01\cdot \text{Capital}}{0.06\cdot \text{premium}\cdot \text{lot size}}\Big\rfloor
\text{Sharpe}=\frac{\bar r_{day}}{s_{day}}\sqrt{252}

Entry and exit rules

BUY CE  bullish pullback
    Buy ATM CE (Δ ≈ +0.5)
    BUY PE  bearish bounce
      Buy ATM PE (Δ ≈ −0.5)
      HOLD when conditions disagree, the RF says no, or a position is already open.

      Two-layer architecture and execution pipeline

      1. 1Data ingestion5-min index CSV
      2. 2Cleaningdedupe, sort, session calendar
      3. 3Indicator engineVWAP, Bollinger, z, d, BW
      4. 4Layer 1 signalCE / PE candidate rules
      5. 5Layer 2 filterRandom Forest p̂ ≥ τ
      6. 6Risk and strikeATM, 1% sizing, target / stop
      7. 7Executionpaper fills, costs, P&L log
      Validation

      Walk-forward: the first 6 sessions are training only. After that the Random Forest is retrained before every session using only signals from earlier days, so every result shown is out-of-sample.

      Honest limits
